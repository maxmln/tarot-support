# Tarot privacy policy

Last updated 28 September 2026.

Tarot is made by Max Bogdanoff. It has no account, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics.

## What stays on your device

Your readings, the cards drawn, your questions and notes, and photos you take of your own cards are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage. They are included in your iPhone backups like any other app data. Deleting a reading deletes its photo. "Delete All Readings" in Settings removes everything.

## What leaves your device

Only the Photograph Cards feature uses the network. When you tap Identify Cards, and again when a reading is written, the app sends the photo (resized) and the question you typed to Replicate (api.replicate.com), which runs an AI model to name the cards and write the reading. This happens only when you ask for it, and only with an API token you provide in Settings. Your token is stored in the iPhone keychain. Replicate's handling of that data is described in its own privacy policy at replicate.com/privacy. Tarot does not send anything else, and does not send the photo anywhere but Replicate.

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server.
