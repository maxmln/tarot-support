# Tarot privacy policy

Last updated 2 October 2026.

Tarot is made by Max Bogdanoff. It has no account, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics.

## What stays on your device

Your readings, the cards drawn, your questions and your notes are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage. They are included in your iPhone backups like any other app data. "Delete All Readings" in Settings removes everything.

## What leaves your device

Nothing. The app does not use the network. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no third-party code.

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server.
