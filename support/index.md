# Tarot support

Questions, problems or ideas: email maxbogdanoff@gmail.com.

## Common questions

**Where is my card of the day?** On the Today tab. Tap the card to turn it over. It stays the same all day; use the menu in the corner of the card to draw a new one for someone else.

**How do I draw a spread?** Tap Draw Cards on Today, or the plus in the tab bar. Type a question if you like, pick a spread, then tap or flick cards from the fan at the bottom. Tap the "i" beside any spread to see what its positions mean.

**How does Photograph Cards work?** Lay your own cards out in reading order, in good light, and take one photo of the whole spread. The app names each card and its orientation; you can correct any of them before saving. It needs a Replicate API token, entered once in Settings under AI Readings. Get one at replicate.com/account/api-tokens.

**Why did two readings share no cards?** By design. Cards drawn earlier in the day are kept out of the next reading, so two people using the same phone get their own cards.

**Where are my readings kept?** Only on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded except, when you use Photograph Cards, the photo and your question to Replicate.
